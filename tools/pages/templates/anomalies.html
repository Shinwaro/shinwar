What the audit found

Three things, one of which was invisible to every test in the project.

Three Threads could never happen Fixed

Salvage Claim, The Passenger and Borrowed Charts were defined, validated, tone-balanced — and referenced by nothing. No option in any Anomaly opened them, so no run could ever contain them. Three of the ten Threads, and three of the four mixed ones, which the design calls the most interesting kind.

The tone test read the pool and happily reported a healthy 30/40/30 split of content half of which was unreachable. It is the same shape as the enemy-mark bug: a registry entry nothing points at fails silently and looks exactly like a thing that has not happened yet. All three now have homes chosen to fit their fiction, and a test asserts the direction that rots — every Thread reachable, every reference real.

An option whose prose promised a deferral it did not have Fixed

The Becalmed's Buy the charts read "sold at the price of being desperate" and paid out instantly. It now opens Borrowed Charts — the Thread named after it, which had no home. The Archive Hulk's rack sale became a claim that takes weeks to clear, and the Lifeboat's tow now carries someone who talks.

Health was worth four times more sold than bought Fixed

Anomalies bought health at an average of 13.4 Alloy a point and sold it back at 3.6, so "sell at every Anomaly, repair at the next Station" was worth about 3.7× on every point traded. That is not a strategy a player discovers — it is a tax on players who do not.

One rate now, both directions: {{SELLRATE}} selling, {{BUYRATE}} buying, a gap of {{RATIO}}×. Max health goes at 15, because it does not come back. Options that also open Coolant Leak are priced on the health they really cost — the immediate loss plus the leak's ten — discounted by 1.5, since a deferred and uncertain loss is not worth the same as one you take now. The Ember Field's Vent coolant to cross was already sitting exactly on that line and is untouched; the rest were fitted to it.

A test now holds every trade inside the band, holds max health near 15, and refuses to let any option borrow more Alloy than Yard Debt collects.
