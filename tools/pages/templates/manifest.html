Cards

By tier, then name. The dashed line under a card is its upgrade — only the parts that change are shown, and a cost circle appears there only when the cost itself moves. {{RARITYMIX}}. By type: {{TYPEMIX}}.

Relics

From Elites and act finales. One per Elite, and a boss offers three of a fixed tier and lets you take one.

{{RELICS}}

Implants

Bought at a Station, or chosen from a boss's three. Max is how many one run may fit — the small ones stack into a build, the run-definers are capped at one so a pile of Alloy cannot simply buy six Energy.

{{IMPLANTS}}

Environments

Shown on the node before you commit to the route, and they change the rules of that fight for both sides. Bosses always fight in Clear Space.

{{ENVS}}

Enemies

Moves are listed in the order their script runs them.

{{ENEMIES}}

Encounters

What actually appears on a node. From row holds an encounter back until the chart has had time to teach you what it needs — a Swarm on the arrival node is not variety, it is a difficulty spike wearing variety's coat.

{{ENCOUNTERS}}

Stance Masteries

Bought at a Station. A Mastery permanently rewrites one stance, so the deck you already have reads differently afterwards.

{{MASTERIES}}

Statuses

Both multiplicative statuses are capped, and the cap is in the data beside the number it caps.

{{STATUSES}}

Keywords

Four. The target is at most fourteen at 1.0 — depth is meant to come from stance and heat recontextualising a small vocabulary, not from more nouns.
