Encounters

what a node actually opens

Sorted by total hull inside each tier, which is the number that says how long a fight lasts. Every Elite in an act is the same size on purpose — routing into one has a fixed price, so a solo Elite and an escorted one have to be the same bet, and the escort comes out of the budget rather than being added on top. From row holds an encounter back until the chart has had time to teach you what it needs.

Enemies

by act, then tier

A turn is the average of the enemy's moves, counting only the attack parts — a plating turn or a buff turn pulls it down, which is right, because those turns do not hurt you. Moves are listed in the order the script runs them.

Environments

{{NENVS}}

Shown on the node before you commit to the route, and the rule applies to both sides. The digits are the acts it can appear in. A boss never rolls its environment — one is already the act's whole argument about your deck, and a rule applied to both sides on top of that decides the hardest fight by something the player never chose. Acts 1 and 2 fix it to Clear Space. Act 3 fixes it to the Radiation Belt, because the last fight is the one that has to be unwinnable by grinding.
